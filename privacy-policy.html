Slash & Sell – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/12/26

Slash & Sell is a game made by LostTells. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Your save game

Your progress, character and settings are saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Playing co-op with a friend

If you choose to play online co-op, your device connects directly to your friend's device. To find each other, both devices briefly contact a public connection service (PeerJS). As with any direct online connection, the two devices can see each other's network (IP) address. The game sends only game information, such as positions, your character's name and look, and actions. We do not store any of it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
